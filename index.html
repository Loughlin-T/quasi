About QuASI

Leading the future of quantum sensing and autonomous systems

Quantum & Autonomous Sensing Innovations (QuASI) Inc. is a cutting-edge consulting firm specializing in advanced geophysical research and quantum sensing technologies. Founded by Dr. Loughlin Tuck, P.Eng, our company bridges the gap between theoretical quantum physics and practical engineering applications.

With extensive experience in defense research, airborne geophysical surveys, and quantum sensor development, we provide specialized consulting services for magnetic anomaly detection, navigation systems, and unmanned aerial vehicle integration.

Our expertise spans multiple disciplines including engineering physics, geology, and geophysics. Based in Russell, Ontario, QuASI serves clients across North America, delivering innovative solutions that push the boundaries of sensing technology and autonomous systems.
